# v2.21.5 - AKILI-SPECS methodology update

Release date: 2026-08-05

### Changed

- **Five field Kaizen lessons (KZ-001…KZ-005, from a downstream project's `/akili-archive` retrospectives) hardened into the methodology's gates.** Each one names a defect the packaged contracts could not see: **(KZ-001)** coverage now closes at **scenario and clause granularity, not requirement ID** — `/akili-specify`'s task quality rules + Verification Checklist and `/akili-validate` Phase 4 require every scenario and `BUT`/`AND IT MUST` clause to be owned by a named task, and a gap may never be discharged by citing a *different* requirement that is satisfied (three scenario-level orphans shipped under a complete-looking ID-keyed traceability table; two were "cleared" against the wrong requirement). **(KZ-002)** a **presence-assertion is not a behavioral proof**, stated artifact-agnostic (test assertion, config key, attribute, *or document clause* — the recurrence that widened the rule): `/akili-specify`, `reviewer.md`, and `tester.md` now require presence-evidence to record what it cannot prove, and declare a property the harness structurally cannot evaluate (jsdom layout/contrast; a checker returning "incomplete" without failing) a gap, never covered — a green test certified a truncation clamp whose classes were all present and whose effect was a no-op. **(KZ-003)** `leader.md` gains *Deferring a check (test the assumption first)*: one bounded probe before any "blocked on stack/login/seed data" deferral — a check sat parked a day on "needs an authenticated session" when the component took plain props and rendered in a throwaway harness, then found two shipped defects within the hour. **(KZ-004)** **Correction Closure**: a correction is applied when the superseded value is gone everywhere, not when the cited site is fixed — `/akili-specify` (new section, every Adjust round), `/akili-execute` (Pivot step 3), and `/akili-validate` (doc corrections) now mandate the two-direction sweep (forward: grep the old value beyond the finding's citation list; backward: grep referrers to the corrected section, which may now assert a falsehood), generalizing the factual-claims sweep `/akili-archive` already runs on root guides. **(KZ-005)** figures are claims: `judgment-day` (v1.7) judges and `/akili-validate` Phase 6 must contrast every count/total/quantity against the other in-scope documents' prose — cross-document agreement is not corroboration, it is often one wrong idea copied forward ("8 duplicate groups" coexisted with "repeats the 11 organizations verbatim" across two documents and a Judgment Day without being caught).

### Fixed

- **The installer removes nested destination symlinks before recursive copies, and every `writeFileSync` destination is guarded** (PR #6, Jules/Sentinel — the first Sentinel finding the evidence upheld, with a twist that upheld the *process* too). The report claimed `fs.cpSync(force)` follows nested destination symlinks; the reviewer's first measurement refuted it on Node 20.11/22.12 (cpSync *replaces* the link there). The tiebreaker was the new **CI symlink probe** (`scripts/ci/install-symlink-probe.js`, now a permanent matrix step): on **Node 22.23.1 — all three platforms — `cpSync` writes THROUGH the nested destination symlink**; the semantics changed silently inside the Node 22.x line. The unmeasured assertion and the single-version refutation were both wrong in opposite directions; only the executed matrix held the truth. `removeTargetSymlinks` verified effective on all 6 combinations; the `writeFileSync` guards (update cache, `settings.json`) are unconditional hardening. Review also removed test fixtures the bot committed to the repo root (`dir2/`, `dir3/` — "secret content") and rewrote the sentinel entry to record measured, version-dependent behavior. **Second post-review bot overwrite**: after the reviewer's corrections were pushed, the bot re-pushed its original commit on top (deleting the probe, re-adding the fixtures, reverting the sentinel rewrite) and the merge carried it in — reverted (`git revert c155c99`), same as PR #5's incident. The rule is now two-for-two: an agent branch is mutable until merge; diff the merge result against the reviewed state before anything else.
